Study Calendar

Choose a track, a start date and the hours you can give each week. The calendar lays out each sprint and exam gate in order. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. The page address keeps your choices, so you can bookmark or share your plan. Sprints you tick as done are saved in this browser only.

Sprint by sprint

Exam costs are US list prices for one attempt each. Sprint weeks are planning estimates built on 8 hours a week, and they scale with your hours. Exam gate weeks stay fixed: they are review time, so book the exam inside that window. Check the official exam pages for current versions before you book.
